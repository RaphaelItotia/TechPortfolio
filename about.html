What I Do

I specialize in collecting, cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing complex datasets to provide actionable insights for business growth. From building robust data pipelines to creating interactive dashboards, I help organizations leverage their data effectively.

Key areas include: ETL processes, SQL, Python for data analysis, and dashboarding with tools like Power BI or Tableau.

I write engaging and informative articles on various tech topics, including data science, artificial intelligence, software development, and emerging technologies. My goal is to simplify complex concepts and share practical knowledge with a broad audience.

This includes tutorials, opinion pieces, and summaries of industry trends, targeting both beginners and experienced professionals.

I actively experiment with various AI models and tools, focusing on practical applications for business and personal use. This involves exploring machine learning algorithms, natural language processing (NLP), and computer vision techniques.

My work includes building small-scale AI prototypes, evaluating model performance, and understanding the ethical implications of AI development.

I design and implement advanced Excel solutions, including complex formulas, VBA macros, and data models to streamline reporting and analysis. I also develop automation workflows using tools like Power Automate or Python scripting to reduce manual effort and improve efficiency.

This service is ideal for businesses looking to optimize their daily operations and decision-making processes.
